ABOUT THE STARBOYZ

The Starboyz are a motorcycle stunt riding group from Akron, Ohio, widely recognized for their role in shaping the early era of street stunt riding. Emerging in the late 1990s and early 2000s, they became one of the most visible and influential teams in the sport, helping to bring motorcycle stunt riding from small local scenes into mainstream awareness.

Origins and Culture

Formed at a time when motorcycle stunt riding was largely underground, the Starboyz gained attention for their distinctive approach — combining technical precision with a strong sense of visual style and group identity. They were among the first riders to regularly film and distribute their performances, documenting wheelies, stoppies, and coordinated group rides on highways and urban roads across Ohio.

Influence and Media

Through early stunt videos and later social media, the Starboyz helped popularize a culture of performance riding that influenced countless riders worldwide. Their name became associated with the rise of organized stunt teams and the transition of the practice from informal gatherings to structured showcases and professional events.

Legacy

The Starboyz are often cited in discussions of modern stunt riding as one of the foundational groups that established its current form. Their contributions extend beyond performance — they helped define the visual and cultural identity of the sport. Although based in Akron, their impact has reached a global audience, and their legacy continues to influence the stunt-riding community today.
