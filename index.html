02 / BEFORE THE FEED, THERE WAS THE FOOTAGE.

OHIO ROOTS.
WORLDWIDE
INFLUENCE.

A crew from Akron. A camera. A different way to ride.

Coming out of Ohio in the late ’90s, StarBoyz helped bring motorcycle stunt riding out of the underground and into the spotlight.

Wheelies. Stoppies. Technical riding with an unmistakable identity. They captured it on film and put it out into the world, helping shape a culture that reached far beyond their hometown.

From the early tapes to the next generation, the legacy lives on every time someone presses play.
